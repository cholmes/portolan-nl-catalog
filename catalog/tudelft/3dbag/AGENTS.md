# 3D BAG

Portolan collection — `tudelft/3dbag`

All ~10.8 million buildings in the Netherlands as detailed 3D models, produced by the [3D Geoinformation Research Group](https://3d.bk.tudelft.nl/) at TU Delft in collaboration with [3DGI](https://3dgi.xyz/). The [3D BAG](https://3dbag.nl/) enriches the official [BAG building registry](https://www.pdok.nl/introductie/-/article/basisregistratie-adressen-en-gebouwen-ba-1) (maintained by Kadaster) with building heights, roof types, volumes, and surface areas derived from the [AHN](https://www.ahn.nl/) national point cloud survey.

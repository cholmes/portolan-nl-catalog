# Portolan NL — Cloud-Native Dutch Geodata

Portolan catalog — `.`

A demonstration of Portolan as cloud-native infrastructure for the disclosure of open geodatasets from Dutch government authorities. Inspired by PDOK (Publieke Dienstverlening Op de Kaart), this catalog provides current and reliable geo-information as GeoParquet, PMTiles, and STAC metadata — open, standards-based, and freely accessible to everyone.

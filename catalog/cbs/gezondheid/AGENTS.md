# Human Health Statistics (Gezondheid)

Portolan collection — `cbs/gezondheid`

INSPIRE-harmonized health statistics for the Netherlands, containing doctor counts and mortality rates per 100,000 inhabitants at the NUTS2 (province) level. This is a small dataset: 12 provinces x 2 indicators = ~24 features. Geometry is MultiPolygon provincial boundaries in EPSG:3035 (ETRS89-LAEA), the European standard grid CRS. Published by CBS (Statistics Netherlands) via PDOK as part of the INSPIRE Human Health theme.

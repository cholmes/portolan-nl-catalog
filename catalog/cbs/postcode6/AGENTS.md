# CBS Postcode6 (Statistics per Postcode)

Portolan collection — `cbs/postcode6`

Statistical data for all 6-digit postcodes in the Netherlands, published by CBS (Statistics Netherlands) via PDOK. Contains 157 attributes covering demographics, housing, energy consumption, income, social security, and proximity to facilities. Data is measured as of January 1st annually from 2015 onwards; some variables (energy, income) reflect full-year figures. Privacy protected: postcodes with fewer than 5 residents or 5 housing units are suppressed (value -99997). Data is supplemented over a 3-year period. Native CRS is EPSG:28992 (RD New / Amersfoort).

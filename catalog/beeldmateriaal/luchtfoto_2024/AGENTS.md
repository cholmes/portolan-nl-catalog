# Luchtfoto 2024 — Aerial Orthophotos

Portolan collection — `beeldmateriaal/luchtfoto_2024`

High-resolution aerial orthophotos from the 2024 Beeldmateriaal Nederland summer campaign, covering 356 tiles across 12 kaartblad sheets in the central Netherlands — from the North Sea coast (Haarlem, IJmuiden) to the Veluwe (Apeldoorn, Harderwijk) and from Alkmaar in the north to Gorinchem and Tiel in the south. Each tile covers one kaartblad (Dutch national map sheet, ~5 x 6.25 km) on the Amersfoort / RD New grid (EPSG:28992) at 25 cm ground sampling distance (GSD), yielding 20,002 x 25,002 pixels per tile. Available as 3-band RGB Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs (COGs) with JPEG compression, 512x512 internal tiling, 6 overview levels (AVERAGE resampling), and baked-in per-band statistics. Twelve tiles in kaartblad 32 (Amersfoort) also have 4-band RGBNIR COGs where the NIR band is extracted from band 1 of the Color InfraRed (CIR) winter capture, so RGB and NIR represent different seasons within the same year. Tiles are organized into sub-catalogs by kaartblad sheet number. Source imagery from Beeldmateriaal Nederland — the Dutch government partnership for aerial photography — distributed as GeoTIFF tiles via GeoTiles (TU Delft).
